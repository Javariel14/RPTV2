# E3A2 — Country Catalog + Pricing (local)

- E3A1 Product/Model/Variant retain tenant-global identity. `catalog_market` opts a structural `market` into commerce; `market_product` links an E3A1 node to one market with a localized label and separate commercial code. Availability is historical and independent of global Product lifecycle.
- E3A2-R1 was **FAIL** despite the initial local verify: same-tenant cross-market reads, no effective operational assignment/admin scope or reassignment, fixed currency onboarding, absent FX/published-vs-derived distinction, and missing market/list lifecycle commands were checkpoint blockers. F1 addresses these without rewriting the base pricing model. R2 was also **FAIL**: catalog evidence used an untyped UUID across Market/MarketProduct, and PriceList INSERT could publish directly.
- Currency is a tenant-controlled ISO registry. A global-scope market administrator registers a missing ISO currency (e.g. CLP) with its standard minor-unit scale as inactive, then activates it. Active markets prevent deactivation of their primary currency. No country-specific code or schema is needed for onboarding.
- Markets start `draft`; a scoped administrator activates only after primary currency is active and an explicit admin market scope exists. `retired` preserves history. Draft MarketProducts and PriceLists can be configured by administration; only active markets provide operational access. F2 makes PriceList INSERT draft-only at contract, application and RLS/trigger levels; draft → active → closed requires market scope plus `pricing.manage`. A create-only actor can record list-definition evidence, not price-entry evidence or publication. Draft/closed lists are not operationally readable.
- One half-open operational assignment per actor is enforced by PostgreSQL exclusion. Reassignment is server-authorized, idempotent, version-checked, closes the prior interval and appends the new assignment with actor/request/reason. Self-switch is denied. Administrative market scopes are separate rows; bounded country/regional scopes and tenant-global capability do not turn users into multi-market operational actors.
- RLS for Catalog, Pricing, availability, entries, market profiles, FX and provenance requires tenant + domain capability + current operational market **or** authorized admin market scope. Pricing adds workspace/object policy. Permissive SourceObservation Trust policies explicitly exclude commerce/FX domains. F2 adds `commerce_subject_type` and database subject validation: Market evidence can authorize only as Market, MarketProduct evidence only as MarketProduct, even when their UUIDs are identical. Availability, price-entry and FX rows retain tenant-safe observation FKs plus guards binding evidence to their exact historical rows. Known foreign-market UUIDs and client-supplied market IDs do not bypass server-derived scope; revocation and reassignment take effect on subsequent requests.
- PriceListEntry remains published market price: exact `numeric(18,4)` with source literal, tax treatment/rate evidence, immutable value and half-open `[valid_from,valid_to)` history. `tax_unknown` never means zero. FX uses market-scoped `numeric(24,10)` rates with source evidence, nonoverlapping half-open validity and preserved prior rows. Conversion returns `DERIVED_REFERENCE`/`officialMarketPrice:false` and never writes a PriceListEntry.
- Evidence uses append-only SourceObservation where externally sourced; manually created markets retain actor/request audit provenance without fabricating an external observation. E3A2 migration remains uncommitted and forward-only relative to checkpointed E3A1; earlier migrations are untouched.
